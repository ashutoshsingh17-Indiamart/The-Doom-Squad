# Sample outputs

Six reproducible synthetic experiment paths. The PDFs are generated by the exact dashboard exporter; the JSON files retain every call, recording key, transcript, evaluator evidence, stage-only table, cumulative table and decision. No provider requests or Supabase writes are made.

| Path | Traffic to B | Calls A / B | Final result | Files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sustained improvement | 20% → 40% → 50% → 50% → 100% | 31 / 19 | Variant B wins | [PDF](growth.pdf) · [Audit JSON](growth.json) |
| Early growth, safety stop | 20% → 40% → 0% | 15 / 4 | Variant B rolled back | [PDF](late-safety.pdf) · [Audit JSON](late-safety.json) |
| Early growth, then retreat | 20% → 40% → 20% → 20% → 20% | 31 / 19 | No clear winner | [PDF](fade.pdf) · [Audit JSON](fade.json) |
| Slow start, then recovery | 20% → 20% → 40% → 40% → 40% | 34 / 16 | No clear winner | [PDF](recovery.pdf) · [Audit JSON](recovery.json) |
| Close comparison | 20% → 20% → 20% → 20% → 20% | 34 / 16 | No clear winner | [PDF](hold.pdf) · [Audit JSON](hold.json) |
| Control performs better | 20% → 10% → 5% → 5% → 0% | 35 / 15 | Baseline A wins | [PDF](reverse.pdf) · [Audit JSON](reverse.json) |

Open a fixed path at `http://127.0.0.1:3000/?pin=1&scenario=<path-id>`. The six IDs are `growth`, `late-safety`, `fade`, `recovery`, `hold`, and `reverse`. Controls still affect the outcome; these paths use the default controls and all three goals.

Regenerate from the app directory with `npm run samples`. Reference timestamps are fixed for reproducibility; no real buyers or calls are represented. Audio URLs are relative to the local dashboard.

Screenshots under [screenshots/](screenshots/) show the actual desktop/mobile journey and PDF pages. [scenario-summary.json](scenario-summary.json) offers a small machine-readable index.
